# EX No.6.2.DESIGN THE VARIOUS PARTS OF A MECHANICAL COMPONENT AND ASSEMBLE
## DATE: 23/11/2026

## AIM: 
To Design the various parts of a mechanical component and assemble it using a bottom-up approach then convert it into the orthographic view

## REQUIREMENTS: 
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 4 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 6 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## COMMANDS USED:
### Align: 
The Align tool allows you to align, distribute or organize selected elements, annotations, tags and text along the axis you specify. Furthermore, the Arrange feature will automatically neatly place your tags around the current view.
The Align plug-in for Autodesk® Revit® can help to save time while producing complex drawings with large sets of annotation.
Just select a few elements and the Align tool will sort them for you.

### Derive:
Insert design elements such as Components, Bodies, Sketches, Work geometry, Flat patterns or parameters from another design.
The inserted elements update with changes to the original design

### Move: Moves the selected face, body, sketch, or construction geometry a specified distance or angle.
Select the objects to modify then specify the distance or angle. Use set pivot to reposition the manipulator

## PROCEDURE:

1. Launch the CAD software on the computer and create a new design or part file in the 3D modeling workspace.
2. Set the default drawing units according to the given engineering drawing (millimetres or specified units) and review the front, side, top, and isometric views carefully.

<img width="482" height="272" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-11-25 at 10 34 00 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4d258078-4e22-4506-a4d8-1c5eb7db821c" />

4. Identify the primary structural components of the chair, including the front legs, rear legs, seat frame, seat surface, backrest supports, and horizontal backrest bars.
5. Select the XY plane (or appropriate base plane) and enter the sketching environment to construct the base layout of the chair legs.
6. Draw two rectangular profiles representing the cross-section of the rear legs using the rectangle and dimensioning tools, maintaining the exact spacing specified in the drawing.

<img width="497" height="550" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-11-25 at 10 34 49 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4c7e0deb-93d2-4fff-8bd4-d7017da6628f" />

8. Apply geometric constraints to ensure both rear leg profiles are aligned and symmetrical, then finish the sketch.
9. Use the Extrude command to raise the two rear leg profiles to the required height, forming the solid rear vertical members.
10. Create a new sketch on the base plane and draw two rectangular profiles for the front legs at the specified distance from the rear legs.
11. Dimension the cross-section and spacing of the front legs accurately, finish the sketch, and use the Extrude command to generate the solid front legs to their required height.

<img width="732" height="552" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-11-25 at 10 38 26 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/32ca50fd-9bab-402d-bd46-e0e428d40c85" />

13. Select the upper inner faces of the legs at the seat level and start a new sketch to construct the seat frame connecting all four legs.
14. Use the line, rectangle, and trim tools to define the closed boundary of the seat frame, apply dimensional constraints, and finish the sketch.
15. Use the Extrude operation to give the seat frame a thickness of 6 units (or as specified in the drawing) to firmly join the front and rear legs.

<img width="488" height="542" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-11-25 at 10 36 13 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e42b9cb3-be65-4e9b-a59a-9ac8a5a34cd1" />

17. Select the top face of the seat frame and create a new sketch for the main seat surface.
18. Draw a rectangular profile matching the outer length and width of the seating area, apply necessary constraints, and extrude it to form a flat, solid seat top.

<img width="307" height="1078" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-11-25 at 10 40 29 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6e0c2a20-bc49-478d-9830-dc70610cc0aa" />

20. Select the side plane or the top face of the rear legs to create a sketch for the backrest supporting members extending upward from the rear legs.
21. Position and incline the backrest supports according to the angle and side-view dimensions shown in the drawing, then extrude them to match the thickness of the rear legs.
22. Create a sketch on the front or rear face of the backrest supports to draw the horizontal backrest bars (slats) at the specified vertical heights.
23. Use the Extrude tool to give the horizontal backrest bars their required thickness, connecting the left and right backrest supports.
24. Select the Fillet tool and apply smooth rounded edges to the seat corners, backrest bars, and outer leg edges as indicated in the engineering drawing.
25. Inspect the completed 3D model using front, side, top, and isometric views, verify all dimensions, apply a wooden material appearance, and save the final CAD file.
